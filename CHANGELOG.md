## Versioning Strategy

- **Major (X.0.0)** — Breaking changes in workflow / structure
- **Minor (1.X.0)** — New prompts, case studies, guides
- **Patch (1.0.X)** — Bug fixes, typo corrections, small tweaks

We aim for **2-3 minor releases per month** based on community contribution rate.
