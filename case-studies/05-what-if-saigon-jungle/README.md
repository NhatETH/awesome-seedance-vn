## 🎨 Grid Storyboard Prompt (Excerpt)

```
A 3×3 grid storyboard (16:9 widescreen aspect, 9 cinematic panels with bold black 
4px borders and 8px white gutters). Post-apocalyptic Saigon reclaimed by tropical jungle, 
beautiful melancholic mood — NOT scary, NOT zombie aesthetic.

SETTING: Saigon, Vietnam, 100 years after human disappearance. 
Tropical jungle has reclaimed urban landscape. 
Recognizable landmarks visible but overgrown. 
Sunrise/dawn light throughout, mist rising, birds returning.

VEGETATION: Authentic Vietnamese tropical species:
- Banyan trees (cây đa) with aerial roots
- Bamboo groves (tre)
- Frangipani (hoa sứ)
- Climbing vines (dây leo)
- Ferns (dương xỉ) on building walls
- Lotus (sen) where there's water
- Palm trees (cây cọ) sporadic
- Moss on stone surfaces

COLOR PALETTE:
- Deep emerald greens (multiple tones for depth)
- Warm golden sunrise light
- Cool shadow blues
- Earth browns of weathered stone/concrete
- Lotus pink accents
- AVOID: gray/dead aesthetic, post-nuclear gloom

Read panels left-to-right, top-to-bottom:

Panel 1: WIDE AERIAL of District 1 Saigon, but the entire downtown is 
green canopy with skyscrapers piercing through like ancient temples. 
Saigon River meanders, mangroves expanded. Dawn mist.

Panel 2: MEDIUM. Notre Dame Cathedral Saigon, twin spires partially covered in vines. 
Front face of cathedral has banyan tree growing through stained glass window. 
Bricks weathered but recognizable.

Panel 3: WIDE 3/4. Saigon Opera House facade, now with trees growing through 
upper-story windows, vines cascading down columns. Stairs covered in moss. 
Tropical bird perched on statue.

Panel 4: MEDIUM. Bến Thành Market clock tower, roof partially collapsed, 
massive banyan tree growing through center, aerial roots descending.

Panel 5: WIDE TILT-UP. Bitexco Financial Tower (helipad shape recognizable), 
exterior covered in climbing vines, jungle reaching halfway up tower.

Panel 6: WIDE LOW-ANGLE. Nguyễn Huệ pedestrian street, completely reclaimed, 
ferns and palms growing through paving stones, water lilies in puddles.

Panel 7: WIDE. Saigon River, mangroves expanded into water, lotuses bloom, 
distant Thủ Thiêm tunnel entrance overgrown. Water clearer than today.

Panel 8: MEDIUM. Sài Gòn train station, ancient locomotive halted on tracks, 
vines and ivy completely covering it. Tracks barely visible through grass.

Panel 9: WIDE HERO ESTABLISHING. Sunrise over reclaimed Saigon. 
Mist parting to reveal landmark silhouettes through jungle canopy. 
Birds rising. Lotus bloom in foreground.

STYLE REQUIREMENTS:
- "The World Without Us" documentary aesthetic + Studio Ghibli "Princess Mononoke" beauty
- Reference: National Geographic post-apocalyptic photography
- Anamorphic widescreen, painterly quality
- Dawn mist + golden hour light + cool shadow gradient
- AVOID: zombie aesthetic, gray gloom, scary horror, sci-fi over-tech
- The mood is BEAUTIFUL melancholic, not horror

ARCHITECTURAL ACCURACY:
- Notre Dame Cathedral Saigon: red brick, twin spires, French Gothic style
- Bitexco: helipad disc shape on top, blue-green glass exterior
- Bến Thành: square clock tower at front, market roof structure
- Saigon Opera House: French colonial neoclassical facade
```

## 📝 Lessons Learned

### What worked
✅ **Specific landmark naming** — "Notre Dame Cathedral Saigon, French Gothic, twin spires" not "old church"
✅ **Authentic Vietnamese vegetation** — listing actual species (banyan, frangipani, lotus) gave authenticity
✅ **"Beautiful, not scary" rule** — avoided horror aesthetic, kept mood meditative

### What didn't work first try
❌ **First version had Western landmarks** — AI defaulted to generic "European cathedral" — fixed by emphasizing "Saigon Vietnam" + reference image
❌ **Bitexco initially looked like generic skyscraper** — added explicit "helipad disc shape on top, recognizable from skyline"
❌ **Vegetation looked too North American** — added "tropical Vietnamese species, NOT temperate forest"

### Key insight
What-If videos work because of **anchor + twist**. Anchor = recognizable landmark. Twist = single magical change. If anchor is unclear, twist loses meaning. Be explicit about landmarks even if it makes prompt long.
