## 🇻🇳 Tại sao template này khác biệt

99% video AI "cổ trang" trên TikTok hiện tại là **AI slop** — generic Asian period look, không phân biệt Trung-Việt-Hàn-Nhật, trang phục sai era, kiến trúc lung tung. Lý do: creator không research, chỉ gõ "ancient Vietnamese woman" và để AI guess.

**Template này yêu cầu bạn research 1 era cụ thể** (Trần dynasty, Lê sơ, Nguyễn cuối) và cụ thể hoá MỌI element. Output: video có chất lượng để dùng cho:
- 🎵 MV nhạc cổ trang (Hoàng Thuỳ Linh, Phương Mỹ Chi style)
- 📺 Web drama lịch sử (Drama 12-30 phút webnovel adaptation)
- 🎬 Short film festival (Liên hoan phim Cánh Diều, Bông Sen)
- 📚 Education content (lịch sử qua 60 giây)
- 💎 Brand storytelling (áo dài brand, jewelry truyền thống)

**Pricing:** 8-50tr VND/video tuỳ độ phức tạp. High-end clients (jewelry brand, Đại Sứ Quán Văn Hoá, festival) trả 30-100tr cho production-grade.

## 📚 Research First — 5 Era Templates

Chọn 1 era cụ thể trước khi làm bất kỳ gì khác. Mỗi era có signature riêng:

### 🏛️ Era 1: Lý dynasty (1009-1225) — "Buổi đầu định hình"

**Aesthetic:** Đơn sơ + Phật giáo influence + influence từ Đại Lý/Tống.

**Signatures:**
- Trang phục: áo giao lĩnh hẹp tay (narrow-sleeve cross-collar), màu chủ đạo nâu-xanh-tím
- Headwear: khăn mỏ rìu, mũ Bình Đính
- Architecture: chùa một cột (Diên Hựu), tháp Báo Thiên, cung điện gỗ lim
- Color palette: nâu đất, xanh rêu, đỏ nâu, vàng đất
- Iconic location: Hoa Lư (Ninh Bình), Thăng Long thành (Hanoi)

### 🏯 Era 2: Trần dynasty (1226-1400) — "Hào khí Đông A"

**Aesthetic:** Hùng tráng + chiến tranh chống Mông Cổ + Phật giáo Thiền Trúc Lâm.

**Signatures:**
- Trang phục: áo giao lĩnh tay rộng, áo Tứ Điên (4-flap robe for nobility), giáp lá tre cho lính
- Headwear: mũ phốc (court hat), khăn vành lụa
- Architecture: Tháp Phổ Minh, đền Trần Nam Định, hành cung Thiên Trường
- Color palette: đỏ son, vàng kim, đen tuyền, xanh lục bảo
- Iconic location: Bạch Đằng (river battle), Yên Tử (mountain)

### 🌸 Era 3: Lê sơ (1428-1527) — "Hồng Đức thịnh trị"

**Aesthetic:** Ảnh hưởng Nho giáo Trung Hoa Minh + nội bộ ổn định + văn học Hán-Nôm.

**Signatures:**
- Trang phục: áo bào dài hoa văn rồng (vua), áo Mãng (mandarin), áo dài Tứ Thân (dân gian)
- Headwear: mũ Cửu Long (vua), mũ Phốc Đầu (quan), khăn vành dây (phụ nữ)
- Architecture: Văn Miếu Quốc Tử Giám, Lam Kinh, Côn Sơn
- Color palette: vàng kim ngự dụng, đỏ son, lam ngọc, trắng ngà
- Iconic location: Hồ Hoàn Kiếm (huyền thoại trả gươm), Lam Sơn

### 🎎 Era 4: Nguyễn dynasty (1802-1945) — "Cố đô Huế"

**Aesthetic:** Influence Pháp cuối kỳ + nghi lễ cung đình phức tạp + Phật-Lão-Nho hỗn dung.

**Signatures:**
- Trang phục: áo Nhật Bình (court ladies), áo dài ngũ thân (cải tiến), áo bào Long Cổn (vua)
- Headwear: mũ Cửu Phượng (hoàng hậu), khăn đóng (đàn ông), nón quai thao (phụ nữ)
- Architecture: Đại Nội Huế, lăng tẩm các vua (Tự Đức, Khải Định)
- Color palette: vàng đại nội, tím Huế (lavender muted), xanh ngọc, hồng phấn
- Iconic location: Ngọ Môn, sông Hương, đèo Hải Vân

### 🌊 Era 5: Champa kingdom (192-1832) — "Vương quốc đã mất"

**Aesthetic:** Hindu-Phật giáo + influence Ấn Độ + kiến trúc gạch đỏ.

**Signatures:**
- Trang phục: sarong (váy quấn), áo cộc, đeo nhiều trang sức vàng-bạc-ngọc
- Hair: búi cao, vương miện kim loại
- Architecture: Mỹ Sơn, tháp Po Klong Garai, tháp Bà Ponagar
- Color palette: đỏ gạch, vàng đồng, xanh ngọc bích, trắng san hô
- Iconic location: Mỹ Sơn (UNESCO), Phan Rang

## 🎬 Master Story Arc — 16 Beats Cấu trúc

Đây là arc structure cho period drama 16-panel. Customize theo era + concept của bạn:

### Act 1 — SETUP (Panels 1-4): Establish world

| Panel | Beat | Camera | Mood |
|-------|------|:------:|:----:|
| 1 | Establishing wide shot — kiến trúc/landscape iconic của era | Aerial / Wide static | Awe |
| 2 | Character A introduction — medium shot, signature pose | Medium static | Identity |
| 3 | World detail — texture/prop close-up (lụa, kiếm, sách, đèn) | ECU / Macro | Intimacy |
| 4 | Character A in world — context wide showing both | Wide tracking | Belonging |

### Act 2 — RISING (Panels 5-8): Tension introduces

| Panel | Beat | Camera | Mood |
|-------|------|:------:|:----:|
| 5 | Character B arrives / event triggers | Medium static | Disturbance |
| 6 | Character A reacts — emotion close-up | CU on face | Internal conflict |
| 7 | Conflict visualized — 2 chars in same frame, opposing positions | Wide 2-shot | External conflict |
| 8 | Stakes raised — symbolic prop / detail showing what's at risk | ECU + slight push | Urgency |

### Act 3 — CLIMAX (Panels 9-12): Peak intensity

| Panel | Beat | Camera | Mood |
|-------|------|:------:|:----:|
| 9 | Decision moment — character A's choice manifests physically | CU + slow push-in | Resolve |
| 10 | Action peak — fight / declaration / movement (slow-mo OK) | Dynamic / Action | Catharsis |
| 11 | Reverse angle — opponent/witness reaction | Reverse CU | Impact |
| 12 | Aftermath frozen — moment of held breath | Wide tableau static | Suspension |

### Act 4 — RESOLUTION (Panels 13-16): Aftermath + theme

| Panel | Beat | Camera | Mood |
|-------|------|:------:|:----:|
| 13 | Movement away — character moves / exits | Tracking back | Release |
| 14 | World returns to its state — environment shot | Static wide | Continuity |
| 15 | Final character moment — last expression / pose | CU profile | Reflection |
| 16 | Theme image — symbolic visual summarizing meaning | Composed wide | Legacy |

## 📋 Concrete Example — "Trần Dynasty Resistance Spirit"

Concept: Female warrior 22 tuổi của nhà Trần chuẩn bị xuất chinh chống Mông Cổ. 16 panels show her transformation từ daughter → warrior trong morning ritual.

### Step 1 — Character Bible

```
Character A: Trần Thị Nguyệt — daughter of nobility, 22 tuổi, sắp xuất chinh

Ethnicity: Vietnamese (Kinh), Northern Vietnam
Build: 158cm, slim athletic, không skinny không gym-built (period appropriate)
Hair: jet black, waist-length, slightly wavy, parted center
Face: high cheekbones, double-lid eyes natural, full lips, oval shape, fair-medium skin
Skin: matte natural with slight luminosity at cheekbones, no makeup visible

WARDROBE (chi tiết theo era):
- Underlayer: áo yếm trắng lụa (white silk underbodice with breast-binding strap)
- Mid-layer: áo giao lĩnh tay rộng, lụa "lĩnh tía" deep purple-red, 
  trim "viền lan can" geometric pattern in gold thread
- Outer: áo tứ điên formal robe, navy-black silk với phoenix embroidery silver
- Belt: lụa hoàng kim (gold-thread sash), tied left side
- Bottom: váy lĩnh xếp pli, same purple as áo giao lĩnh
- Footwear: hài cong tip-curved, brocade with phoenix motif

ACCESSORIES:
- Trâm cài: jade hairpin (ngọc bích) carved with chrysanthemum
- Vòng cổ: bạc đeo cốt ngọc lục bảo (silver chain with emerald pendant)
- Kiếm: longsword Đông A style, dragon-engraved hilt, scabbard lacquer with mother-of-pearl

POSTURE/GESTURE:
- Head always slightly tilted up, never bowed (warrior pride)
- Hands: when still, left hand always on sword hilt
- Walking: straight back, deliberate steps, không vội vã

VOICE/PERSONALITY:
- Quietly determined, not loud
- Eyes: lock onto subject and don't waver
```

### Step 2 — 16 Beats Outline

```
PANEL 1 (Setup - Establish world):
WIDE AERIAL: Hành cung Thiên Trường at dawn, mist rising from Đại Việt countryside.
Tower Phổ Minh in distance. Birds rising. Cool blue dawn light.

PANEL 2 (Setup - Character intro):
MEDIUM static. Trần Thị Nguyệt standing in courtyard, full ceremonial outfit.
Faces camera direct, hand on sword hilt. Confident pose. Still as statue.

PANEL 3 (Setup - World detail):
ECU MACRO. Her hand brushing the dragon-engraved sword hilt.
Detail of jade pendant against silk. Morning light catches mother-of-pearl scabbard.

PANEL 4 (Setup - Character in world):
WIDE TRACKING. Nguyệt walks through stone courtyard. Camera moves with her.
Servants kneel as she passes. Cherry blossoms (mai trắng) drift past.

PANEL 5 (Rising - Disturbance):
MEDIUM static. Older man (her father, war advisor) appears from inner palace.
Holding scroll in both hands. Expression grave. He stops 3m from her.

PANEL 6 (Rising - Reaction):
CU on Nguyệt face. Eyes hold steady. Lips press slightly. 
A single drop of moisture catches morning light at corner of eye but does not fall.

PANEL 7 (Rising - Conflict):
WIDE 2-SHOT. Father offers scroll. Nguyệt extends hand. They mirror each other.
Stone tablets with Đông A characters frame them on both sides.

PANEL 8 (Rising - Stakes raised):
ECU + slight push. Her hand opening the scroll. Brush calligraphy reads:
"Quốc gia hữu sự" (When the nation is in trouble). Light breeze ruffles paper edge.

PANEL 9 (Climax - Decision):
CU + slow push-in. Her face. Eyes close briefly. Open with clear resolve.
A single tear escapes her right eye and tracks down to jaw.

PANEL 10 (Climax - Action peak):
DYNAMIC. Slow-motion. She draws the sword in single fluid motion, 
holds it vertical in front of face, kisses the blade once.
Cherry blossoms suspended in air around her.

PANEL 11 (Climax - Reverse angle):
REVERSE CU. Father's face from behind sword tip. His expression: pride mixed with grief.
A nod, almost imperceptible. He says nothing.

PANEL 12 (Climax - Held tableau):
WIDE STATIC. Both stand frozen in courtyard. She: sword vertical, ready stance.
He: hand over heart, head bowed. Cherry blossoms stop falling. 
Time appears suspended.

PANEL 13 (Resolution - Movement away):
TRACKING BACKWARD. She walks away from camera toward the gate.
Father stays in foreground, watches her go. She does not look back.

PANEL 14 (Resolution - World continuing):
WIDE STATIC. Hành cung courtyard empty now. 
Cherry blossom petals on stone where she stood. Wind picks up petals.

PANEL 15 (Resolution - Final character):
CU PROFILE. Nguyệt on horseback now, crossing wooden bridge.
Profile against rising sun. Hair flows behind. Sword visible at her side.

PANEL 16 (Resolution - Theme image):
COMPOSED WIDE. Her silhouette small in vast landscape — mountains of Yên Tử in distance.
She rides toward a sunrise that paints sky in gold and red.
Banner of Trần dynasty (3 dragons) stands tall ahead in landscape.
```

### Step 4 — Motion Prompt (Seedance, 3-Layer Structure)

```
STYLE & MOOD:
Vietnamese period drama, Trần dynasty 1383, photo-realistic cinematic with anamorphic lens.
Color script evolves: cool dawn blue → warm amber → saturated red-gold climax → muted resolution.
Natural motivated lighting. Wong Kar-wai meets Trần Anh Hùng aesthetic. 16:9, 24fps.

DYNAMIC DESCRIPTION:
Animate this 16-panel period drama into a continuous 18-second cinematic sequence. 
Camera works through the day's emotional journey:

At 0-2s (panels 1-2): Slow aerial descent over palace at dawn, then push into Nguyệt 
standing still in courtyard. Cool mist, ambient temple bells.

At 2-4s (panels 3-4): Macro on her hand on sword, then track with her as she walks 
through courtyard, cherry blossoms drift. Soft footsteps on stone, silk rustling.

At 4-7s (panels 5-7): Father appears, scroll offered, the moment of receiving. 
Static cuts between medium and close-up. Wind picks up. Distant wood-block percussion.

At 7-9s (panels 8-9): Macro on calligraphy reveal, then push into her face for the decision.
Single drum strike on the tear release. String section enters.

At 9-12s (panels 10-12): The sword draw in slow-motion, reverse to father's face, 
hold the tableau. Cherry blossoms freeze mid-air. Drums and strings peak.

At 12-15s (panels 13-15): She walks away, courtyard empties, then she's on horseback. 
Strings sustain, gentle horse hooves. Wind picks up.

At 15-18s (panel 16): Final wide pull-back showing her silhouette small in vast landscape, 
riding toward golden sunrise with Trần banner ahead. Score swells then resolves to single flute note.

STATIC DESCRIPTION:
ULTRA-CONSISTENT character identity — Trần Thị Nguyệt's face, hair, wardrobe identical 
in every frame. Sword and jade pendant present always. Đại Việt 1383 setting unchanged. 
Cherry blossom motif persists. Lighting key from camera-right at 45° angle locked. 
NO Chinese opera aesthetic. NO Korean sageuk. Distinctly Vietnamese Trần dynasty.

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- No joint biomechanics issues (sword draw must be fluid, not glitchy)
- No reflection inconsistencies on sword/scabbard
- No face morphing across cuts — face is LOCKED to reference
- Continuous spatial logic — courtyard architecture stays oriented same way
- Cherry blossoms physical accuracy (gravity, drift speed)
```

### Step 5 — Iteration Strategy

#### First pass (480p Fast, 3 versions)
Generate 3 variants với:
- V1: Default prompt above
- V2: Add `slower pacing, hold each panel longer, 22-second total`
- V3: Add `more dramatic cherry blossom density, dreamlike quality`

Pick best.

#### Issues to fix in iteration 2
- **If face drifts panel 9-16:** Add stronger anchor `Panels 9-16 face MUST match panels 1-8 EXACTLY. NO morphing.`
- **If sword draw glitches:** Replace `single fluid arc` with `slow controlled draw — sword exits scabbard in 0.5s with no clipping or texture glitch`
- **If cherry blossoms too sparse:** `Dense cascade of cherry blossoms — at least 30 visible petals at any time during panels 4, 10, 12`
- **If color script doesn't evolve:** Be explicit per panel-block trong static description

#### Final 1080p generation
Run the winning prompt at 1080p Standard. ~$2-3 cost.

## 🎵 Audio / Music Pairing

Period drama đòi hỏi audio đúng era. Suggestions:

### Score
- **Nhã nhạc cung đình Huế** (Royal Court Music UNESCO) — for Nguyễn era
- **Hát quan họ Bắc Ninh** instrumental — for Lê / Trần era
- **Trống đồng Đông Sơn** percussion — for ancient eras
- **Composers:** Hoàng Cương, Lê Cát Trọng Lý (modern with traditional spirit)

### Foley
- Wood-block percussion for wood doors / footsteps on stone
- Silk rustling (record real silk fabric)
- Distant temple bells (chuông đồng)
- Wind through bamboo
- Horse hooves on dirt road

### Mixing
- LUFS: -16 (cinematic, room for dynamic range)
- High-pass at 80Hz to remove room noise
- Reserve center for dialogue/breath
- Spatial: wide stereo for ambient, narrow for action moments

## 🚨 Common Mistakes to Avoid

### ❌ Mistake 1: Generic "ancient Asian" aesthetic
**Cause:** Prompt không cụ thể era, AI default về Chinese aesthetic.
**Fix:** Always specify era + location + wardrobe pieces by name.

### ❌ Mistake 2: Anachronistic mix
**Cause:** Mix Trần dynasty hair với Nguyễn dynasty áo dài.
**Fix:** Lock 1 era. Research từng accessory.

### ❌ Mistake 3: Khiếm nhã / không đúng nghi lễ
**Cause:** Pose không phù hợp era (vd: nữ noble cười tươi như modern selfie).
**Fix:** Reference real period paintings/photos. Posture = controlled, head up, không cười toe.

### ❌ Mistake 4: Architecture lung tung
**Cause:** Mix kiến trúc Trung Hoa Tử Cấm Thành với Vietnamese palace.
**Fix:** Specify đúng building name (Đại Nội Huế, Văn Miếu, Hành cung Thiên Trường).

### ❌ Mistake 5: Music sai era
**Cause:** Dùng Chinese erhu music cho Việt period.
**Fix:** Nhã nhạc Huế / quan họ / đàn nguyệt phù hợp era.
