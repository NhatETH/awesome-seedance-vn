## 🎬 The 16-Beat Brand Story Arc

Luxury TVC không phải product showcase — đây là **brand narrative**. 16 panels cấu trúc thành 4 emotion blocks:

### Block 1 (Panels 1-4): WORLD — establish universe
| Panel | Beat | Purpose |
|-------|------|---------|
| 1 | Aerial / wide world | Set the universe (city, landscape, scale) |
| 2 | Architectural detail | Show craftsmanship of environment |
| 3 | Texture / material macro | Hint at quality (raw material) |
| 4 | Process moment | Show creation / craftsmanship in progress |

### Block 2 (Panels 5-8): PROTAGONIST — introduce ideal customer
| Panel | Beat | Purpose |
|-------|------|---------|
| 5 | Character entering world | Hero arrives in environment |
| 6 | Lifestyle moment | Their daily life, aspirational |
| 7 | Detail of their world | Watch on wrist, jewelry, setting |
| 8 | Decision / gaze moment | Looking at something off-screen |

### Block 3 (Panels 9-12): PRODUCT REVEAL — the hero shot
| Panel | Beat | Purpose |
|-------|------|---------|
| 9 | Product establish wide | Product in environment, in scale |
| 10 | **Hero shot CU** | THE iconic product image, beauty pass |
| 11 | Macro texture | Detail that proves quality |
| 12 | Product in action | Used / worn / lived-in moment |

### Block 4 (Panels 13-16): EMOTION — brand promise delivered
| Panel | Beat | Purpose |
|-------|------|---------|
| 13 | Character + product | Owner satisfaction, intimate moment |
| 14 | Lifestyle elevated | Their world transformed by ownership |
| 15 | Iconic brand image | Logo + product + atmosphere |
| 16 | Tagline / final image | Memorable closing, brand mark |

## 📋 Concrete Example — "Premium Jewelry Brand TVC, Áo Dài Heritage Collection"

Concept: Luxury jewelry brand launches "Heritage" line — necklaces inspired by Trần dynasty motifs but made for modern wear. Target: women 28-45, household income 100tr+/month. 30-second TVC for digital + cinema.

### Step 1 — Brand & Product Bible

```
BRAND POSITIONING: "Heritage reimagined" — traditional motifs, modern execution.
NOT vintage / retro / costume. Modern luxury that hints at heritage subtly.

PRODUCT: Phoenix necklace
- 18K rose gold chain, fine bone-thin links
- Pendant: hand-carved jade lục bảo (emerald jade) shaped as stylized phoenix tail
- Setting: rose gold filigree inspired by Trần dynasty cloud patterns
- Total length: 45cm necklace, pendant 3cm
- Weight: 12g (light enough for daily wear)
- Price tier: 80-150 triệu VND retail

VISUAL CODE:
- Primary palette: rose gold, deep jade green, ivory white, soft black
- Typography: serif elegant (Didot / Bodoni style), gold foil treatment
- Photography: Mario Sorrenti / Paolo Roversi inspiration — soft, intimate, painterly
- AVOID: harsh studio light, busy backgrounds, mall-jewelry aesthetic

NO BRAND NAME used in this template (anonymized for portfolio).
In real production: replace "[BRAND]" with client name, "Heritage" line name with theirs.
```

### Step 2 — Character Bible

```
CHARACTER A: WOMAN, 36 tuổi
Vietnamese, urban professional aesthetic.

Build: 168cm, slim elegant, refined posture
Hair: dark brown with subtle highlights, mid-length cut to collarbone, 
slight wave, parted side, polished glossy finish (think L'Oreal commercial)
Face: 36-year-old natural beauty, fine lines starting (NOT youthful 20s look),
warm tan-gold undertone skin, bare-faced makeup with lipstick

WARDROBE for this TVC:
- Modern áo dài (designer cut, not traditional) — silk dupioni in deep ivory
- Cut: high mandarin collar, fitted bodice, slim sleeves to mid-forearm, 
  side slits to mid-thigh
- Color: deep ivory with subtle warm undertone (NOT pure white)
- No prints, no embroidery on the áo dài (let necklace be hero)
- Quần lụa: matching ivory silk, wide-leg cut, falls just above ankle
- Shoes: nude pointed-toe heel, subtle gold buckle (just visible peeking)

STYLING:
- Hair: half-up half-down with discrete gold pin
- Makeup: glowy skin no foundation, bronzed cheek, brown smoky eye soft, 
  matte rose-nude lip
- Jewelry: ONLY the phoenix necklace + small gold stud earrings (tiny, not competing)
- Watch: discreet vintage piece on wrist, gold + brown leather (heritage hint)

PERSONALITY/POSTURE:
- Grace, restraint, gravitas — NOT bubbly or smiling broadly
- Half-smiles, knowing glances, contemplative gazes
- Body language: confident stillness, deliberate movements
- Eyes: kind but composed, NEVER wide-eyed surprised
```

### Step 6 — Multi-Engine Composite (Pro Technique)

For luxury TVC, single image model isn't enough. Use composite:

#### Stage A: Layout (GPT Image 2)
Generate 4×4 grid với prompt above. Use to lock composition + character identity.

#### Stage B: Character upgrades (Nano Banana 2)
Regenerate panels 5, 6, 7, 8, 13, 14 (character close-ups) trong Nano Banana 2 với same prompt — gets better Asian skin texture + hair detail.

#### Stage C: Product hero shot (FLUX 1.1 Pro)
Regenerate panel 10 (THE hero shot) trong FLUX 1.1 Pro với detailed product description — gets best material texture (jade subsurface, gold anisotropic).

#### Stage D: Composite in Photoshop
Combine best cells từ each engine into final 4×4 grid. Use as reference for Seedance.

**Why this matters:** Each model có strength khác. Mix gives result that single model can't achieve. Đây là kỹ thuật mà 99% creator không biết → bạn có moat.

#### Stage E: Seedance generation
Use composite grid as reference. Generate 480p Fast 3 versions → pick best → 1080p Standard final.

## 🎨 Beauty Pass Technique — The Real Secret

What separates "AI luxury" from "actual luxury": **beauty pass on materials**.

### Material physics in prompts

**Silk fabric:**
```
Silk: directional thread weave visible in close-up, 
soft sheen with anisotropic reflection following thread direction, 
subtle drape physics with weight, no static-cling artifacts.
```

**Jade stone:**
```
Jade: subsurface scattering at edges (light penetrates 2-3mm into stone),
internal cloudiness visible in deep areas, 
polished surface reflects with specific jade-green hue tinting,
hand-carved tool marks subtly visible at macro level.
```

**Rose gold metal:**
```
Rose gold: anisotropic brushed surface, light reflection follows brush stroke direction,
warmer hue than yellow gold (slight pink), highly polished areas mirror-like 
while brushed areas catch light as soft gradient.
```

**Skin (luxury beauty):**
```
Skin: subsurface scattering visible on cheeks and ears (warm undertone glow),
individual eyelashes sharp at macro, fine vellus hair on face visible in highlight,
no plastic-doll texture, natural pore structure preserved.
```

**Hair (commercial beauty):**
```
Hair: individual strand definition at edge, glossy reflection following hair flow,
no helmet-hair stiffness, natural movement physics, 
fly-aways at edges are realistic not glitchy.
```

Apply 4-5 of these in static description for cells với material focus.

## 🚨 Common Mistakes

### ❌ Mistake 1: TVC feels generic / "stock luxury"
**Cause:** No specific brand voice or cultural rooting.
**Fix:** Always cite specific reference (Hermès / Cartier / Louis Vuitton) + cultural angle (Vietnamese heritage / French colonial fusion / etc.).

### ❌ Mistake 2: Product looks fake / CGI
**Cause:** Missing material physics descriptors.
**Fix:** Use beauty pass texture descriptors above.

### ❌ Mistake 3: Visual too busy
**Cause:** Trying to fit too much in 30s.
**Fix:** Restraint. Less is more. Hero product gets 5+ seconds of screen time.

### ❌ Mistake 4: VO space crowded
**Cause:** Visuals don't allow for voiceover breathing.
**Fix:** Build "VO breathing space" into pacing (panels 1-2, 8-9, 16 are quiet).

### ❌ Mistake 5: Brand name too prominent
**Cause:** Logo in every cell.
**Fix:** Logo only in panel 16. Cinema doesn't need logo on every shot. Trust the brand voice.
