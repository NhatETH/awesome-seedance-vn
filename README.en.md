## 🎯 Why this repo

While English resources for Seedance 2.0 are abundant, the Vietnamese AI video community has been underserved. This repo bridges that gap with bilingual content, localized case studies, and Vietnam-specific market insights — useful for both VN creators and global audiences interested in Asian market techniques.

We aggregate the best practices from EvoLinkAI, Atlas Cloud, Higgsfield, ZeroLu, top X creators (@MrLarus, @Saccc_c, @LudovicCreator), and real production workflows from Vietnam-based agencies.
