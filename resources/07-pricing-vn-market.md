## 💡 Pricing Strategy

### Tier 1 — Beginner (0-3 tháng kinh nghiệm)
- Pricing: **floor of the table** (Min column)
- Goal: Build portfolio, accumulate testimonials
- Volume target: 20-30 videos/tháng
- Income tiềm năng: **15-25 triệu VND/tháng**

### Tier 2 — Intermediate (3-12 tháng)
- Pricing: **Mid column**
- Goal: Specialize 1-2 niches
- Volume: 15-20 videos/tháng
- Income: **40-80 triệu VND/tháng**

### Tier 3 — Expert / Agency (12+ tháng)
- Pricing: **Max column**
- Goal: Premium clients, retainer deals
- Volume: 8-12 videos/tháng + 2-3 retainers
- Income: **100-300 triệu VND/tháng**

## 🎯 Niche-Specific Insights

### 🛍️ E-commerce / Affiliate (Volume Play)
- **Reality:** Brand bán hàng trên TikTok Shop yêu video VN-style
- **Sweet spot:** **1.5tr/clip × 50 clip/tháng = 75tr/tháng**
- **Top clients:** Shopee Mall sellers, brand mỹ phẩm Hàn-Nhật, F&B
- **Best platform:** 0ai.vn (rẻ, scale)
- **Pro tip:** Build brand-level template, reuse character bible cho 10-20 video

### 💄 Beauty / Skincare (Premium)
- **Reality:** Beauty brand WILLING TO PAY for cinematic
- **Sweet spot:** ASMR macro 3tr/clip + TVC 15tr/30s
- **Top clients:** Innisfree, Laneige, local Korean-style brands
- **Best format:** ASMR Macro #24 + cinematic close-up
- **Pro tip:** Show beauty AGENCY case study trên portfolio

### 🏠 Real Estate (Highest ARPV — average revenue per video)
- **Reality:** BĐS willing to pay 15-30tr/walkthrough video
- **Sweet spot:** Architecture walkthrough 15s + drone establishing
- **Top clients:** Vinhomes, Masterise, Sunshine, Nam Long
- **Best format:** What-If conceptual + walkthrough realistic
- **Pro tip:** Niche này 80% chưa biết AI video — first mover advantage huge

### 🎵 K-pop / V-pop MV (Highest creativity ceiling)
- **Reality:** Idol agency Saigon paying 5-30tr cho 15-30s segments
- **Sweet spot:** Grid 16-panel dance + idol consistency
- **Top clients:** SHOWBIZ Saigon, Agency 8th Avenue, indie label
- **Best format:** Grid 4×4 dance sequence
- **Pro tip:** Train 3-5 idol Character Bibles → sản xuất batch

### 🍜 F&B / Food (Volume + ASMR)
- **Reality:** Restaurant chain order 10-20 videos/tháng
- **Sweet spot:** ASMR macro 1.5-3tr/clip + drone establishing 2tr/clip
- **Top clients:** Phúc Long, The Coffee House, Highlands, Pizza chain
- **Best format:** ASMR macro food + lifestyle B-roll
- **Pro tip:** Combo pack 5 videos giá 8tr (thay vì 10tr) → close deal nhanh

### 🎓 EdTech / Course (Recurring revenue)
- **Reality:** Online course creator cần intro/outro/B-roll cinematic
- **Sweet spot:** Retainer 10tr/tháng cho 5-8 video
- **Top clients:** Học viên Vexere, Topica, Funix, indie YouTuber edu
- **Best format:** Documentary B-roll + What-If conceptual
- **Pro tip:** Retainer model = stable income, predictable

## 📈 Margin Analysis

### Cost per video (production)
- Generate Seedance 480p test (3-5 versions): **~$1**
- Generate Seedance 1080p final (1 version): **~$2-3**
- GPT Image 2 storyboard: **~$0.20-0.40**
- **Total cost: ~$3-4 per finished video**
- **In VND: ~80-100K**

### Revenue (selling)
- Min tier: 500K → margin: **~83%**
- Mid tier: 1.5tr → margin: **~94%**
- Max tier: 5tr → margin: **~98%**

### Time investment
- Iteration cycle: **15-30 phút**/video
- Editing in CapCut: **10-15 phút**/video
- Client back-and-forth: **15-20 phút**/video
- **Total: ~45-60 phút per finished delivered video**

### Hourly rate equivalent
- Tier 1: **500K-1tr/hour** (volume model)
- Tier 2: **2-4tr/hour** (premium)
- Tier 3: **5-10tr/hour** (agency tier)

## 🎓 How to Charge More

### 1. Position as DIRECTOR, not "AI generator"
**Bad:** "Tôi làm video bằng AI, 500K/clip"
**Good:** "Tôi giúp brand kể câu chuyện qua video cinematic, AI là tool. Pricing 3-15tr/dự án."

### 2. Bundle deliverables
- Single TVC: 8tr
- Bundle (3 versions: 9:16 + 16:9 + 1:1): 12tr (50% premium for repurpose)
- Bundle (5 episodes drama): 30tr (avg 6tr/ep, lock client)

### 3. Charge for storyboard separately
- Storyboard pre-approval: 2-3tr (client buy-in)
- Production (after approval): 8-15tr
- This makes total feel justified

### 4. Add post-production polish
- Color grading in DaVinci: +30%
- Custom sound design: +20-50%
- Original music license: +20-100%

### 5. Retainer / monthly contracts
- 5 videos/month: 30-50tr (locked rate)
- 10 videos/month: 50-80tr
- Predictable income, scale via batch production

## 🚫 What NOT to do

❌ **Race to bottom on Fiverr/Upwork** — destroys VN market for everyone
❌ **Promise unlimited revisions** — set 2-3 revision limit
❌ **Skip contract** — even small jobs need 1-page agreement
❌ **Underprice friends/family** — they'll spread your "low price"
❌ **Reveal pricing publicly** — keep it custom-quote per client

## 🤝 Negotiation Tips

### When client says "đắt quá"

❌ Don't: Drop price 50% immediately
✅ Do:
- "Em hiểu budget, mình có thể giảm scope đến đâu để fit budget?" (open conversation)
- "Bundle 3 video giá 25tr (thay vì 27tr cho 3 video lẻ) — anh có cần thêm 2 video không?"
- "Anh có thể trả 50% giờ + 50% sau 30 ngày không, em xếp slot tuần sau"

### When client compares with overseas freelancer ($5/video Fiverr)
✅ "Đó là Fiverr global. AI sinh trên đó không scale được brand quality như cinematic VN. Bài test free em làm cho anh xem trước, anh judge."

### When client requests unlimited revisions
✅ "Em include 2 vòng revision miễn phí. Sau đó mỗi revision 500K — đảm bảo chất lượng cao + thời gian em khỏi quá tải."
