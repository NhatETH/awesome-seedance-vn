## 🎯 Concept

Macro extreme close-up của skincare product. Hand approaching, opening, applying. Audio = 50% of the experience (ASMR sounds critical). Beauty pass on materials (gel viscosity, skin texture, glass refraction).

Best with FLUX 1.1 Pro reference image of:

## 🎬 Motion Prompt (3-Layer)

```
STYLE & MOOD:
12-second ASMR macro skincare video. K-beauty clean clinical luxury. 
9:16 vertical for TikTok. 24fps. Soft cool palette.
Reference: Mario Sorrenti macro work meets Sephora hero shots.

DYNAMIC DESCRIPTION:
9-panel grid storyboard animated as continuous 12-second sequence:

0-3s: ECU. Closed jar on marble surface, fingers approaching from frame edge,
slow deliberate movement. Audio: silence, then soft glass-tap.

3-5s: Lid being twisted off, mechanical detail visible. 
Audio: subtle glass-on-glass twist, pop of seal release.

5-7s: MONEY SHOT — finger touches gel, surface tension stretches,
single thread of gel briefly stretches and breaks. SLOW MOTION x 0.5.
Audio: satisfying squelch + brief stretch sound.

7-9s: Gel on fingertip, finger moves to back of opposite hand.
Audio: silence (anticipation), then soft gel-against-skin sound.

9-12s: Gel spreads on skin, glossy finish reveal, 
subsurface scattering visible on skin.
Audio: gentle smoothing sound, ambient room tone, soft exhale.

STATIC DESCRIPTION:
Hand identity LOCKED: Korean female hands, fair-pale skin, 
short natural nails clear topcoat only, slim long fingers. 
NO rings, NO accessories, NO color polish.

Product LOCKED: Frosted glass jar 50g, jade-green translucent gel,
brushed silver lid. Same product cross all frames.

Lighting LOCKED: Soft top key, gentle gradient shadows, 
slight blue-cool color temperature throughout.

Macro lens character: same depth of field, same focus distance.

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Hands clearly anatomically correct, 5 fingers always visible
- Gel physics realistic: surface tension, viscous flow, no glitches
- No motion blur on extreme macro shots (clarity is the appeal)
- Sound design ASMR-quality: clean, isolated, satisfying
- Skin pore detail preserved at macro (no plastic-doll smoothing)
- No visible brand logo (anonymized for affiliate use)

AUDIO (CRITICAL — 50% of ASMR appeal):
- 0-3s: Silence with light room tone, glass-tap on contact
- 3-5s: Subtle twist sound, satisfying pop
- 5-7s: Squelch + stretch sound (THE ASMR moment)
- 7-9s: Anticipation silence, soft skin contact
- 9-12s: Smooth spreading sound, gentle exhale finale
```

## 🎯 Variations

### Variation A: Toner pad
Replace gel with cotton toner pad. Focus on sweep-across-skin motion. 
Audio: soft brushing sound + light wet pad sound.

### Variation B: Serum dropper
Replace jar with dropper bottle. Hero shot = serum drop falling onto fingertip in slow motion.
Audio: soft squeeze of dropper, drop landing.

### Variation C: Cream jar
Replace gel with thick cream. Different texture physics — cream holds shape, doesn't stretch.
Audio: scoop sound, satisfying tactile spread.

### Variation D: Sheet mask reveal
Pull sheet mask out of pouch, unfold, application motion.
Audio: package crinkle, wet adhesion sound.

## 🚨 Common Issues

### Issue 1: Gel looks too plastic-like
**Cause:** Material physics not specified.
**Fix:** Add "subsurface scattering, internal viscosity hint, slight bubbles, water-like translucency"

### Issue 2: Hand identity changes mid-video
**Cause:** Multi-shot drift.
**Fix:** Reference image of hand at start. Include "Same hand, same skin tone, same nails throughout entire video."

### Issue 3: Audio feels artificial
**Cause:** AI-generated audio sometimes flat.
**Fix:** Mix AI audio with real foley sounds in CapCut/DaVinci. ASMR community tells the difference.

### Issue 4: Macro focus shifts unexpectedly
**Cause:** Auto-focus drift in motion gen.
**Fix:** Specify "Focus locked at fingertip distance throughout, no rack focus shifts"
