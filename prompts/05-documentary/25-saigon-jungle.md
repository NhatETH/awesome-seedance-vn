## 🎯 Concept

What-If: 100 năm sau con người biến mất, Saigon bị rừng nhiệt đới chiếm lại. Mood post-apocalyptic nhưng đẹp (NOT scary, NOT zombie). Recognizable landmarks (Notre Dame, Bến Thành, Bitexco) phải nhìn được rõ qua vegetation.

---

## 🎨 Reference Setup

Generate 1-2 establishing shots first với GPT Image 2:
- Aerial Saigon District 1, jungle canopy + skyscrapers piercing through
- Notre Dame Saigon front view với vines covering spires

Use these as reference for video gen consistency.

## 🎬 Motion Prompt (3-Layer)

```
STYLE & MOOD:
15-second contemplative documentary establishing — 
post-apocalyptic Saigon reclaimed by tropical jungle, 100 years after humans disappeared.
Beautiful melancholic mood — NOT scary, NOT zombie aesthetic, NOT post-nuclear gloom.
Reference: "The World Without Us" documentary + Studio Ghibli "Princess Mononoke" beauty.
24fps. 16:9 master, will crop to 9:16 for TikTok.

DYNAMIC DESCRIPTION:
Continuous 15-second sequence flowing through reclaimed Saigon at sunrise:

0-3s: AERIAL ESTABLISHING. Drone shot over District 1 Saigon. 
Entire downtown is green canopy. Skyscrapers pierce through like ancient temples. 
Saigon River meanders, mangroves expanded into water. Dawn mist rising.
Camera slowly descends.

3-6s: CINEMATIC PUSH-IN. Notre Dame Cathedral Saigon, twin red brick spires partially 
covered in climbing vines. Massive banyan tree growing through stained glass window 
of front face. Camera pushes in slowly toward central rose window.

6-9s: REVEAL TILT-DOWN. Bến Thành Market clock tower (square tower, recognizable shape), 
roof partially collapsed, massive banyan tree growing through center, 
aerial roots descending. Tropical birds circling. Camera tilts down 
revealing market interior reclaimed by ferns and palms.

9-12s: WIDE TRACKING. Bitexco Tower (helipad disc shape recognizable on top), 
exterior covered in climbing vines. Jungle reaches halfway up tower. 
Camera tracks slowly past in wide shot. Lotus blooms in foreground puddles.

12-15s: HERO ESTABLISHING. Wide pull-out reveal — sunrise over the entire 
reclaimed Saigon. Mist parting to show landmark silhouettes through jungle canopy. 
Birds rising in V-formation. Lotus pink accents in foreground. 
Final beat hold for 1.5 seconds (thumbnail moment).

STATIC DESCRIPTION:
Setting LOGIC: Same Saigon throughout — landmarks remain in their real geographic 
positions relative to each other. Saigon River always east of downtown. 
Sun rises east through entire video.

VEGETATION CONSISTENT — same Vietnamese tropical species throughout:
- Banyan trees (cây đa) with aerial roots
- Bamboo groves (tre) sporadic
- Frangipani (hoa sứ) in bloom
- Climbing vines (dây leo) on architecture
- Ferns (dương xỉ) on stone surfaces
- Lotus (sen) in water bodies
- Palm trees (cây cọ) sporadic

LIGHTING LOCKED: Sunrise golden hour throughout — warm gold from east, 
cool blue shadows in west, mist rising in valleys. NOT changing time of day.

COLOR PALETTE LOCKED:
- Deep emerald greens (multiple tones for depth)
- Warm golden sunrise light
- Cool shadow blues
- Earth browns of weathered stone/concrete
- Lotus pink accents

HARD CONSTRAINTS:
- Landmarks recognizable: Notre Dame Cathedral red brick twin spires, 
  Bitexco helipad disc on top, Bến Thành square clock tower, 
  Saigon River shape preserved
- Vegetation authentic Vietnamese tropical (NOT temperate forest, NOT North American)
- Mood beautiful melancholic NOT horror NOT zombie NOT gray gloom
- No humans, no zombies, no decay aesthetic
- Architecture weathered but structural integrity visible (NOT crumbled to ruins)
- No anachronisms (no modern vehicles, no signage, no satellite dishes)
- Smooth camera movement (no shake, no glitches)

AUDIO:
- 0-3s: Wind through canopy, distant birds awakening
- 3-6s: Soft mist sounds, occasional stone-shift creak from cathedral
- 6-9s: Tropical bird chorus building, leaves rustling
- 9-12s: Gentle wind, water lapping (mangroves)
- 12-15s: Full dawn chorus peak, single distant temple-bell-like resonance, 
  contemplative ambient music swell
```

## 🎯 Variations

### Variation A: Hà Nội underwater (Atlantis-style)
Replace Saigon với Hà Nội Old Quarter underwater. 
Hồ Gươm expanded. Coral instead of vines. Fish swimming through narrow streets.

### Variation B: Hội An time-jump to Champa era
Replace overgrown with Champa kingdom era. 
Same Hội An buildings but with Cham architectural elements, ancient era setting.

### Variation C: Hạ Long Bay frozen ice age
Famous limestone karsts but covered in ice/snow. 
Mammoths in distance. Aurora in sky.

### Variation D: Mekong Delta in distant future
Mekong but with futuristic floating city ruins reclaimed by water.

## 🚨 Common Issues

### Issue 1: AI defaults to generic "European cathedral"
**Cause:** Western architectural bias in training.
**Fix:** Be VERY explicit: "Notre Dame Cathedral Saigon, French Gothic, twin red brick spires" + reference image.

### Issue 2: Vegetation looks North American
**Cause:** Generic "forest" defaults to temperate.
**Fix:** Add "Vietnamese tropical species — banyan, frangipani, lotus, bamboo, palm. NOT pine, NOT oak, NOT maple."

### Issue 3: Mood feels scary instead of beautiful
**Cause:** "Post-apocalyptic" triggers horror aesthetic.
**Fix:** Add "Beautiful melancholic mood, NOT zombie horror, NOT scary. Studio Ghibli aesthetic, NOT The Last of Us."

### Issue 4: Bitexco looks like generic skyscraper
**Cause:** AI doesn't know specific building shape.
**Fix:** Specify "Bitexco Financial Tower, helipad disc shape on top (recognizable from skyline), blue-green glass exterior."
