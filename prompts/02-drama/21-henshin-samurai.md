# Prompt #21: Henshin — Saigon Người Việt → Samurai

> **Format:** HENSHIN (transformation)
> **Aspect:** 9:16 vertical
> **Duration:** 8s
> **Difficulty:** Intermediate

---

## 🇻🇳 Brief tiếng Việt

Format đỉnh điểm tháng 5/2026 trên TikTok #seedance. Người Việt 28t mặc thường ngày → biến hình thành samurai cinematic, khuôn mặt giữ NGUYÊN. Phù hợp cho cosplay creator, gaming influencer, brand "biến đổi mạnh mẽ", campaign "glow up".

**Tại sao viral?**
- Wow factor cao — viewer "Sao biến hình mượt vậy?!"
- Format sharing-friendly (mọi người tag bạn bè)
- Algorithm autoplay yêu — replay 3-5 lần

## 🇬🇧 English Prompt (copy this)

```
@Image1 (a 28-year-old Vietnamese man in white t-shirt and dark jeans) stands in a modern Saigon street at golden hour. The camera slowly pushes in from medium shot to close-up.

At 0-3s: man stands still, looking at camera with calm expression. Light breeze in his hair. Cars blur in background.

At 3-5s: a burst of golden light surrounds him from above. Particles swirl around his body in slow motion. His expression intensifies.

At 5-7s: his outfit transforms into full samurai armor — black lacquer chest plate with red accents, helmet with curved horns, katana sheathed at his hip. Cherry blossoms begin falling around him.

At 7-8s: final hero pose with right hand on katana hilt, intense determined gaze. Camera locks. Wind blows cherry blossoms across the frame.

ULTRA-IMPORTANT: The face stays EXACTLY identical to @Image1 throughout — same facial features, skin tone, hair color, eye color. Only the clothing transforms. No face morphing.

Cinematic dramatic lighting, golden hour with magical glow effect. Anamorphic lens flare. Shallow depth of field. 9:16 vertical, 24fps, 8 seconds.
```

## 🐛 Common Issues & Fixes

### Issue 1: Face changes during transformation
**Fix:** Add stronger emphasis at the end of prompt:
```
The face is LOCKED. ZERO face morphing. Skin tone, eye color, nose shape, mouth shape — all identical to reference image at every frame.
```

### Issue 2: Transformation too sudden / jump cut
**Fix:** Extend transformation phase, add intermediate state:
```
At 3-4s: golden light begins forming around shoulders.
At 4-5s: light wraps around chest, beginning to solidify into armor plates.
At 5-6s: full armor crystallizes, helmet forms last.
```

### Issue 3: Cherry blossoms too sparse / too dense
**Fix:** Adjust quantity in prompt:
- Sparse: `a few cherry blossoms drift past the frame`
- Medium: `cherry blossoms fall steadily around him`
- Dense: `dense cascade of cherry blossoms fills the frame`

### Issue 4: Camera too aggressive / shaky
**Fix:** Replace `slowly pushes in` với `static medium shot, no camera movement` for grounded feel.

## 🧪 Iteration Tips

Use 4-step iteration loop:

1. **Baseline**: Generate 3 versions ở 480p Fast với prompt gốc
2. **Single Variable**: Pick best, change ONE thing (camera OR transformation timing OR clothing)
3. **Score**: 1-10 cho continuity, instruction adherence, post-prod usability
4. **Final**: Generate winner ở 1080p Standard
